What is Embedded Tutoring?

Embedded Tutoring is a form of academic support in which a tutor actively and continually interacts with students during — and outside of — class throughout the entire term. Rather than tutoring being something you seek out separately, your tutor becomes a regular part of the course itself, helping to clarify concepts and encourage engagement and participation as the semester unfolds.

This is a new offering for PSY 225, developed in collaboration with the University Learning Center.
